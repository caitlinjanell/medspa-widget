1. Who We Are

Meet Goldie, LLC ("Meet Goldie," "we," "us") provides an AI-powered pre-consultation widget that aesthetic practices ("Providers") embed on their websites for prospective clients ("End Users"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices.

When an End User completes a Provider's consultation widget, we collect and process that information on the Provider's behalf to deliver the consultation to that Provider. The Provider's own privacy practices govern how it uses the information after receiving it.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate a personalized consultation summary for the End User and deliver it to the Provider the End User chose;
  • To notify Providers that a new consultation was received;
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service, including preventing abuse;
  • To manage Provider accounts, verify NPI numbers, and process subscriptions; and
  • To understand use of our marketing pages, if you accept analytics cookies.

We do not sell personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not use End User consultation information or photos to train AI models.

4. Photos

At least one facial photo is required to complete a consultation, because the AI analysis depends on it. End Users who do not wish to share photos can contact the Provider directly instead.

Photos are collected only after the End User checks a consent box agreeing to AI analysis, sharing with the Provider, and storage for 14 days. We record when that consent was given. Photos are analyzed by our AI service provider, shown to the Provider in its secure dashboard, and automatically deleted from our systems 14 days after submission. We do not use photos to identify individuals or create facial recognition templates, and photos are not used for medical diagnosis.

5. How We Share Information

With the Provider. Consultation information is shared with the Provider whose widget the End User used. Lead alert emails and texts to Providers contain only the End User's first name and last initial; full details are available only in the Provider's secure dashboard.

6. Data Retention

  • Photos: automatically deleted 14 days after submission.
  • Other consultation information: kept while the Provider's account is active, until the End User or Provider deletes it.
  • Provider accounts: kept until the Provider deletes the account; all associated consultation data is then permanently deleted.
  • Billing records: kept by Stripe and as required for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Technical logs: kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

7. Your Choices and Rights

End Users can delete their consultation information right after submitting it using the "Delete my data" link in the widget, or by emailing us at hello@meetgoldie.com with the Provider's name and the email address used. Information already delivered to the Provider must also be deleted by the Provider; please contact them directly.

Providers can view and update account information in the dashboard, delete individual leads, and delete their account at any time.

Depending on where you live (including California, Washington, and other states with consumer privacy or health-data laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to appeal a decision about your request. To make a request, email hello@meetgoldie.com. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Where we process information on a Provider's behalf, we may refer your request to that Provider.

Cookies: you can accept or decline analytics cookies at any time using the Cookie Preferences link in our footer. We honor Global Privacy Control signals. See our Cookie Policy.

8. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls, rate limiting, and security headers, and we limit what is sent by email and text message. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of a breach as required by law.

9. HIPAA

Meet Goldie is not a HIPAA covered entity. Providers represent to us that they do not use the Service in connection with services billed to health insurance or government health programs, so Meet Goldie does not act as a HIPAA business associate. The consultation widget is not a medical records system. See Section 8 of our Terms of Service.

10. Children

The Service is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date and, for material changes, notify Providers by email or in the dashboard.
