Privacy Policy

Meet Goldie — Service-Specific Privacy Disclosures

1. What We Collect

Meet Goldie collects two categories of information:

Provider accounts: Name, email address, NPI number, clinic name, and billing information for aesthetic practices that create an account.

Consultation preferences: Information submitted by end users through an embedded widget — including name, contact information, aesthetic goals, areas of interest, prior aesthetic experience, budget range, and optional photos. This information is collected on behalf of the deploying practice and is not used by Meet Goldie for any purpose other than providing the service.

2. How We Use It

Consultation preference data is used solely to generate a personalized aesthetic consultation experience and to deliver that information to the deploying practice. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use end-user consultation data to train AI models.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide the service, consultation preference data (including optional photos) is processed by Anthropic (AI analysis) and stored via Railway (cloud infrastructure). Both services are subject to their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies. Billing is processed by Stripe, which handles payment data independently under its own privacy and security standards.

4. Photos

Photos submitted through the widget are optional and used only to personalize the aesthetic consultation experience. They are transmitted to the deploying practice and processed by Anthropic's AI for aesthetic observation purposes. Photos are not used for medical diagnosis, are not stored as medical records, and are not shared with any party other than the deploying practice and Anthropic for the purpose described above.

5. This Is Not a Medical Records System

Information collected through Meet Goldie's consultation widget is aesthetic preference data, not Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA. Meet Goldie is not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate with respect to consultation preference data. Providers using Meet Goldie remain independently responsible for their own HIPAA obligations with respect to their patients.

6. Data Retention

Consultation preference data is retained as long as the provider's account is active. Providers may delete individual leads from their dashboard at any time. Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@meetgoldie.com.
