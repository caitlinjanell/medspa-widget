1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, like browser local storage, work the same way. This policy covers both.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. The consultation widget that practices embed on their websites does not use analytics cookies.

3. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies and storage are needed for the site to work and stay secure, for example keeping you signed in or protecting against attacks. They can't be turned off in our settings, but you can block them in your browser; parts of the site may then stop working.

4. Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics on our marketing pages (such as the home page and sign-up page) to understand how visitors find and use the site. Analytics cookies are off by default and load only if you click "Accept" in our cookie banner. IP addresses are anonymized. Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Choices

You can change your choice at any time: open Cookie Preferences. You can also use the Cookie Preferences link in our footer.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as declining analytics cookies and won't show the banner. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

6. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if the cookies we use change. Questions: hello@meetgoldie.com.
