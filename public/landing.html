Features

Everything your front desk
can't do at 2am

Meet Goldie captures, qualifies, and nurtures leads around the clock — with the warmth and intelligence of your best staff member.

🧠
AI Treatment Recommendations
Personalized modality suggestions based on each client's goals, concerns, history, and budget — not a generic brochure.
📷
Photo Vision Analysis
Clients upload facial photos and receive an AI-powered visual skin assessment before they ever walk through your door.
💬
AI Virtual Concierge
After receiving their plan, clients can chat with your AI guide — which already knows their profile — to ask questions and book.

Automatic Lead Capture

Fully Branded
Matches your clinic's colors, logo, and tone. Clients feel like they're in your hands from the very first interaction.

Works Everywhere
Embeds on any website with a single line of code. Mobile-optimized. No app download required for clients.
