Accessibility Statement

Meet Goldie is committed to making our website and consultation widget usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we do

  • Form fields have visible, programmatically linked labels.
  • All controls can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Text meets minimum color-contrast requirements.
  • Status and error messages are announced to screen readers.
  • Animations are reduced when your device requests reduced motion.
  • Pages can be zoomed up to 200% without loss of content.

Known limitations

Clinics can choose their own accent color for the consultation widget, which may not always meet contrast guidelines. Photo analysis results are generated by AI and presented as text.

Feedback and assistance

If you have trouble using any part of this site or the consultation widget, or need information in a different format, email us at hello@meetgoldie.com. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
