Meet Goldie — Service-Specific Terms

1. Nature of the Service

Meet Goldie is an aesthetic consultation preference tool designed to help aesthetic practices collect client goals, interests, and aesthetic background prior to an in-person consultation. The platform is a business productivity and client engagement tool, not a medical records system, electronic health record (EHR), or clinical decision support system.

2. No Medical Advice

Nothing provided through Meet Goldie — including AI-generated treatment suggestions, aesthetic observations, or consultation summaries — constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. All outputs are informational and for consultation personalization purposes only. All treatment decisions are made solely by the licensed provider during an in-person evaluation.

3. No Provider-Patient Relationship

Use of Meet Goldie does not create a provider-patient relationship between Meet Goldie, LLC and any end user. The provider-patient relationship exists solely between the client and the licensed aesthetic provider at whose practice the widget is deployed.

4. Not a Medical Records System

Information collected through Meet Goldie — including aesthetic preferences, goals, experience background, and facial photos — is collected for the purpose of personalizing the aesthetic consultation experience. This information does not constitute a medical record and is not intended to be used as one. Meet Goldie is not a HIPAA covered entity or business associate with respect to information collected through the consultation preference widget, as such information does not constitute Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA.

5. Provider Responsibility

Providers who deploy Meet Goldie on their websites are responsible for ensuring their own compliance with applicable laws and regulations, including any obligations they may have under HIPAA or other healthcare privacy laws with respect to their patients. Meet Goldie provides a consultation preference collection tool and does not assume responsibility for the provider's independent legal obligations.

6. Photos

Photos submitted through the widget are used solely to generate personalized aesthetic observations for that consultation session. They are shared with the deploying practice and used with a third-party AI service for aesthetic analysis purposes. Photos are not used for medical diagnosis and are not stored as medical records.
